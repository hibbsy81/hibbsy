St Bartholomew’s Church

St Bartholomew’s Church serves the community of Barbon and Middleton and the surrounding area. It has an ecumenical tradition with services led by both Anglican and Methodist clergy and lay people. We meet every week for worship at 11:00 on Sunday morning.
