Rules and keys
  • Pick nine letters, then make the longest word you can before the clock runs out.
  • Each letter tile can be used once. When the clock stops you can still finish your word, then submit it.
  • One point per letter. A nine-letter word scores 18.
  • Words are checked against a British English word list, so very rare words may be rejected.
  • Keys: V and C pick a vowel or consonant while dealing. During the round type letters to build your word, Backspace deletes, Enter starts the clock or submits.
